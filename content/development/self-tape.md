## Record your voice at your own pace

I want to hear your honest conversation about the place you're part of. Not a polished answer - just you, thinking out loud, on your phone, in your own time. What follows are suggestions; do what you can.

Answer what you're comfortable with, but please answer the three questions marked *. You can ask to stay anonymous, ask for parts not to be used, or withdraw before the final project is shared. Full details are in the [release form](/development/release-form/).

## Where & when

- **Where:** outside somewhere quiet (woods, park, garden, quiet street) or a quiet room at home. Avoid busy cafes and anywhere you can't speak freely.
- **When:** when you have energy - 45-60 minutes, uninterrupted. Morning or early evening usually beats late night.

## Setting up

- **iPhone:** use Voice Memos. In Settings → Voice Memos → Audio Quality, choose Lossless. Afterwards you can tap the recording → three dots → Enhance Recording to cut background noise.
- **Android:** use your phone's built-in recorder, or Google Recorder (free).
- Turn on **Do Not Disturb** so notifications don't interrupt.
- Hold the phone 6-8 inches from your mouth, or set it on a table angled towards you - away from surfaces that resonate, and without your hand over the mic.
- Outside in wind? Move somewhere sheltered.
- Before sending, listen back to 10 seconds. If it's muffled, try a different position.

## The questions

Any order. They're prompts, not rules.

1. **\* Who are you?** - Your name, where you live, your role here. How did you end up here?
2. **\* What is this place?** - Not the official description - your version. What does it feel like?
3. **What brought you here?** - A moment, a person, a conversation? What were you looking for?
4. **What's hard?** - What takes energy from you? What would you change? A specific example helps.
5. **What makes you happy in life, not only here?** - A person, a moment, a feeling, a food. Be specific. This one is about you.
6. **What would you tell someone thinking about this?** - What do you wish you'd known at the start?
7. **\* Is this the future you were dreaming of?** - Take your time. Be honest about the gap between the dream and the reality.
8. **What would keep you here?** - Optional. What would need to change for you to stay long-term?
9. **What do you dream for this place in 10 years?** - Optional. Not realistic - just imagine.

Real answers are messy, contradictory, emotional. That's what I want. If you say "I love this" and also "I'm exhausted", both are true - that's where the material is. Stumble, pause, start a sentence over. No one's listening live.

## Sending it

Email the file (.m4a on iPhone, .mp3 on Android) to [info@enricopolicardo.com](mailto:info@enricopolicardo.com) with the subject "This Was Tomorrow - Self-Tape [your name or Anonymous]". Too large to email? Use Google Drive, Dropbox or iCloud Mail Drop.

Include this line in your message:

"I agree to have this recording used for the 'This Was Tomorrow' project as described in the release form. [Your name or 'Anonymous']"

Questions before you record? Just email me.
