## What happens during a visit

Part of This Was Tomorrow, an Arts Council England-supported photography research project.

I'm a photographer and artist researching how people organise and sustain collective living. I work slowly and deliberately, spending time in places to understand how they actually function.

I'm not here to write about you, validate you, or turn you into a case study. I photograph places - interiors, shared spaces, material traces of how you live together. That's it.

## How I work

I photograph places, not people as primary subjects. I focus on details - how spaces are adapted, what's maintained, what's broken. Shared areas. Material traces of daily life.

If people appear in images, they're part of how the place functions - elements within the environment. Not the focus.

I don't stage anything. I observe, walk around, look closely. The pace is slow.

## Recording & consent

I might record some conversations. This is always optional. If I want to record, I'll ask directly. You can say no. You can stop at any point. No consequences.

Audio and photographs are kept completely separate. You'll never hear someone's voice explaining a photograph, and you'll never see a photo being "illustrated" by someone's voice.

Full details about consent and usage are in the [release form](/development/release-form/).

## Questions I might ask

Some of the questions that might come up, depending on who I'm talking to and what feels right in the moment:

1. **Who are you?** - Tell me who you are - resident, member, worker, supporter. No need for your full name or details you don't want to share. Something like "I am Enrico, and I live on a canal boat in London".
2. **Is this the future you were dreaming of?** - Give yourself time to think about this one. There's no right answer.
3. **What would you tell someone thinking about doing something like this - building something that feels radical from the outside?** - What do you wish someone had told you at the start?
4. **What - or who - brought you here?** - What moment, person, or conversation made you decide this was worth doing?
5. **What is something that truly makes you happy - about this, or about life in general?** - I ask this to everyone. It's not a test. I'm curious what you actually enjoy.
6. **What is special about this place - something you feel, or something you'd want me to see when I visit?** - Not what's on the website. What's real here?
7. **What do you miss, or what's hard - here, in this way of living or working?** - The honest one. What costs you? What would you change if you could?

## After I leave

You'll receive professional photographs. These are yours to use however you want - your website, archives, events, displays. Full usage rights. The work may also appear in exhibitions in 2026-27, potentially at places I visit and during Open House Festival in London. You'll be credited and involved in how it's presented.
