## Do you have a story?

I'm looking to hear from people who live in or run a community-led housing project, cohousing scheme, Community Land Trust or cooperative - anywhere in the UK, or any other way of living together, whether it happened in the past, is happening now, or only exists as an idea.

If that's you - or you know someone I should speak to - [get in touch](mailto:info@enricopolicardo.com). For the background to the project, see [About](/about/).

## What a visit involves

I spend a few days in a place, sometimes longer. The commitment from you is light - I'm here to observe and be present, not to consume everyone's time. Once we've touched base, I'll propose dates. Everything is negotiable.

I'd rather stay somewhere that allows reflection - independent spaces, local guesthouses or community spaces, not chain hotels. Suggestions welcome; I have a small budget to contribute.

The [Field Guide](/development/field-guide/) explains what happens on the ground. If a visit isn't possible, the [Self-Tape Guide](/development/self-tape/) lets you record your voice at home.

## What you get back

Professional photographs and any recorded audio, yours to use freely - website, archives, events, local displays. I share images soon after a visit; the finished body of work takes longer, but you won't need to wait for it.

The work will be exhibited in 2026-27, potentially at places I visit and during Open House Festival. You'll be credited and involved in how it's presented.

## First conversation

We can talk on [Jitsi](https://meet.jit.si/thiswastomorrow) - free, open-source, and not owned by Google, Microsoft or Zoom. Use that link when we first connect, and we'll decide together how to proceed.
