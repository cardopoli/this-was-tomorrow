## What happens during a visit

I'm not here to write about you, validate you, or turn you into a case study. I photograph places - interiors, shared spaces, what's adapted, what's maintained, what's broken. If people appear, they're part of how the place works, not the focus.

I don't stage anything. I observe, walk around, look closely. The pace is slow.

## Recording & consent

I might ask to record a conversation. It's always optional: you can say no, or stop at any point, with no consequences.

Audio and photographs are kept apart. You'll never hear a voice explaining a photograph, or see a photograph illustrating a voice. Full details are in the [release form](/development/release-form/).

## Questions I might ask

Depending on who I'm talking to and what feels right in the moment:

1. **Who are you?** - Resident, member, worker, supporter. No need for your full name. Something like "I am Enrico, and I live on a canal boat in London".
2. **Is this the future you were dreaming of?** - Take your time. There's no right answer.
3. **What would you tell someone thinking about doing something like this?** - What do you wish someone had told you at the start?
4. **What - or who - brought you here?** - What moment, person or conversation made you decide this was worth doing?
5. **What truly makes you happy - about this, or about life in general?** - I ask everyone. It's not a test.
6. **What is special about this place?** - Not what's on the website. What's real here?
7. **What's hard?** - The honest one. What costs you? What would you change?

## After I leave

You'll receive the photographs, with full rights to use them however you want. You'll be credited and involved in how the work is presented.
