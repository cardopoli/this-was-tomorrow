## Record your voice at your own pace

Part of This Was Tomorrow, an Arts Council England-supported photography research project.

I want to hear your voice - your honest conversation about this place you're part of. Not a polished answer. Just you, thinking out loud.

You can record this yourself, at home, in your own time. Use your phone. Use your words.

And remember the following are guidelines and suggestions that make the interview optimal for me, but please just do what you can!

## What I'm recording

Audio interviews. Conversations about how you came to this place, what works, what's hard, what you dream about. That's it.

## Privacy & consent

Full details are in the [release form](/development/release-form/). But here's the short version:

- Just reply to what you feel comfortable with (but there are a couple of questions I really need from you, and I will mark them with an asterisk *).
- Your recording might be used in exhibitions, publications, or played back to communities.
- You can request anonymity or ask that parts not be used.
- You can withdraw anytime before the final project is shared.

## Where & when

**Ideal places:** Outside (woods, park, garden, quiet street) - nature absorbs sound. Or a quiet room in your home.

**Time:** When you have energy and focus. 45-60 minutes of uninterrupted time. Morning or early evening often work better than late night.

**Avoid:** Busy cafes, anywhere with constant background noise, anywhere you feel watched or can't speak freely.

## Technical setup - iPhone (Voice Memos)

Before you start:

- Open Settings → scroll to Voice Memos → Audio Quality → select Lossless (better quality)
- Swipe down from top right → Do Not Disturb (toggle on) to stop notifications interrupting you
- Hold your phone 6-8 inches from your face (like a phone call), or place on a flat surface 6-8 inches away
- Make sure the microphone (bottom of phone) is not blocked

Recording:

- Open Voice Memos app
- Tap the large red circle to start
- Speak naturally - pause to think, it's fine
- Tap red stop button when done
- Optional: Tap the recording → three dots → toggle Enhance Recording on (reduces background noise)
- Share via Mail, or use Google Drive / Dropbox / iCloud Mail Drop if the file is too large

## Technical setup - Android (Google Recorder)

Before you start:

- Use the built-in recorder that comes with your phone or download Google Recorder from Google Play Store (free, made by Google)
- Pull down notification shade → find Do Not Disturb → toggle on
- Hold your phone 6-8 inches from your face, or place on flat surface 6-8 inches away
- Make sure microphone is not blocked by your hand or case

Recording:

- Open your recorder (or Google Recorder)
- Tap the large red microphone button to start
- Speak naturally - pause when you need to think
- Tap red stop button when done
- Send it to my email, or use Google Drive / Dropbox if the file is too large

## Audio quality tips

- **Phone position:** 6-8 inches from your mouth. Don't cup your hand around the mic (blocks it). If on a table, angle it toward you and try not to put the phone on surfaces that might create a resonance.
- **Speak clearly but naturally.** Not like you're performing. Pause to think. Don't rush. Breathe normally.
- **Wind noise:** If recording outside and wind is loud, move to a sheltered area or try indoors instead.
- **Background noise:** Some is okay. Keep it reasonable. Use "Enhance Recording" on iPhone to reduce it if needed.
- **Before sending:** Listen back to 10 seconds. Make sure the audio is clear and you can hear yourself. If muffled, try recording again in a different position.

## The questions

You don't need to answer in order. Answer what feels natural. Questions are prompts, not rules. But please reply to those with an asterisk * next to them.

1. **\* Who are you?** - What is your name? Where do you live? How would you describe this place - are you a resident, member, worker? And how did you end up here? What was your story of arrival?
2. **\* What is this place?** - How would you describe it to someone who'd never heard of it before? Not the official description - your version. What does it feel like?
3. **What brought you here?** - Was there a moment, a person, a conversation that made you decide this was worth doing? What were you looking for?
4. **What's hard?** - What do you miss? What takes energy from you? Be honest. What would you change if you could? Give a specific example if you can.
5. **What makes you happy in life, not only here?** - Something that genuinely makes you feel alive. A person, a moment, a feeling, a food. Be specific - not abstract. This question is about you.
6. **What would you tell someone thinking about this?** - If someone was considering something like this, what would you tell them? What do you wish you'd known at the start? Your honest advice.
7. **\* Is this the future you were dreaming of?** - Take your time with this one. There's no right answer. Be honest about the gap between your dream and the reality.
8. **What would keep you here?** - Optional. What would it take for you to stay long-term? What would need to change?
9. **What do you dream for this place in 10 years?** - Optional. Not realistic - just imagine. What would you want it to be?

## After you record

**Email to:** [info@enricopolicardo.com](mailto:info@enricopolicardo.com)

**Subject:** "This Was Tomorrow - Self-Tape [Your Name or Anonymous]"

**Attach:** Your voice memo file (.m4a on iPhone, .mp3 on Android)

**Can't email because the file is too large?** Send it via Google Drive, Dropbox or iCloud Mail Drop.

Include this line in your message:

"I agree to have this recording used for the 'This Was Tomorrow' project as described in the release form. [Your name or 'Anonymous']"

## Final notes

**You're not performing.** You're thinking out loud. Real answers are messy, contradictory, emotional. That's what I want.

**Honesty beats perfection.** If you stumble, pause, change your mind - that's authentic. Keep it.

**The gap between dream and reality is the story.** If you say "I love this" and also "I'm exhausted" - both are true. That's where the material is.

**Take your time.** Pause. Think. Start a sentence over. No rush. No one's listening live.

**Questions?** Email me back. I'm happy to clarify before you record.
